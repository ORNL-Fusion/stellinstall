264 ! Also, estimate mean elongation of plasma from the following relations
265 ! for an axisymmetric torus with elliptical cross section and semi-axes
266 ! a and a * kappa (kappa >= 1)
267 !
268 ! surf_area _p = 2*pi*R * 2*pi*a ctwiddle(kappa_p)
269 ! volume_p = 2*pi*R * pi*a ** 2 * kappa_p
270 ! cross_area _p = pi*a ** 2 * kappa_p
271 !
272 ! The cirumference of an ellipse of semi-axes a and a * kappa_p is
273 ! 2 * pi * a ctwiddle(kappa_p)
274 ! The exact form for ctwiddle is 4 E(1 - kappa_p^2) / (2 pi), where
275 ! E is the complete elliptic integral of the second kind
276 ! (with parameter argument m, not modulus argument k)

278 ! The coding below implements an approximate inverse of the function
279 ! d(kappa) = ctwiddle(kappa) / sqrt(kappa)

281 ! kappa = 1 + (pi^2/8) * (d^2+sqrt(d^4-1)-1)
