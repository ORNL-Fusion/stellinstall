subroutine, public siesta_bfield::update_bfield ( logical, intent(in)  l_add_res)

Routine (both parallel, serial) for updating B^s, B^u, B^v for both ideal and resistive perturbations.

Advances half-mesh magnetic field components in nm space from time t to t + dt. Used to update values of BSUPMNH and BSUPIJH stored in quantities module.
