subroutine siesta_bfield::update_bfield_par ( logical, intent(in)  l_add_res)

Parallel routine for updating B^s, B^u, B^v for ideal (and resistive) perturbations.

Advances half-mesh magnetic field components in nm space from time t to t + dt. Used to update values of BSUPMNF and BSUPIJF in quantities module.
